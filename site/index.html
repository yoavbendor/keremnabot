KeremNaBot 🌱

A demo chatbot answering from a knowledge graph + ontology built over 26 translated human-rights / policy reports on the West Bank. Every quote it shows you is a verbatim excerpt found by exact text search in the source documents — never LLM-invented.

Your key is stored only in this browser (localStorage) and sent directly, per-request, to a relay that forwards it to Anthropic—never logged, never stored server-side. You pay for your own usage (typically a few cents for a full session). See console.anthropic.com to get a key.
